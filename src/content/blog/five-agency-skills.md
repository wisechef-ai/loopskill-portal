## The Agency Stack Has a Gap

You've got project management (ClickUp), CRM (HubSpot), accounting (Xero), and social scheduling (Buffer). But between "client asks for something" and "it's done," there's a lot of manual work that eats into margins.

AI agent skills fill that gap. Not chatbots — *executable skills* that run on your machine, connect to your data, and produce deliverables.

Here are the five highest-ROI skills for agencies from the WiseChef library.

## 1. Client Reporter (Free)

**What it does:** Pulls GA4, Google Search Console, Meta Ads, and TikTok Ads data into a branded PDF report.

**Why agencies need it:** The average agency spends 2-4 hours per client per month on reporting. With 10 clients, that's 20-40 hours — half a week of someone's time.

**ROI math:** At $50/hr, Client Reporter saves $1,000-$2,000/month for a 10-client agency. It's free forever.

[Read the full guide](/blog/automate-client-reporting)

## 2. Proposal Builder (Operator tier)

**What it does:** Generates custom proposals from your templates, pulling in client-specific data, case studies, and pricing.

**Why agencies need it:** Proposals are the lifeblood of new business. But writing them from scratch every time is a 4-6 hour drag. Proposal Builder turns that into 30 minutes of review.

**ROI math:** If you send 5 proposals/month and each takes 5 hours, that's 25 hours. Proposal Builder cuts it to 2.5 hours. At $50/hr, that's $1,125/month saved — and proposals go out faster, which means higher close rates.

## 3. SEO Audit Runner (Cook tier)

**What it does:** Runs technical SEO audits on any website — crawl issues, Core Web Vitals, meta tags, schema, internal linking.

**Why agencies need it:** SEO audits are a standard deliverable that agencies either charge for or use as lead magnets. Either way, producing them manually takes 3-5 hours per site.

**ROI math:** Sell 4 SEO audits/month at $500 each = $2,000 revenue. With SEO Audit Runner, your cost to produce is under an hour of review time.

## 4. Cold Outreach Automation (Operator tier)

**What it does:** Researches prospects, writes personalized outreach emails, and sends them via your existing email infrastructure.

**Why agencies need it:** Most agencies rely on referrals. Cold outreach works, but it requires consistent volume and personalization — exactly what AI agents excel at.

**ROI math:** Sending 50 personalized emails/week with a 5% reply rate = 10 conversations/week. At a 20% close rate and $2,000 MRR/client, that's $4,000 MRR added per month from outreach alone.

## 5. Competitive Intelligence (Cook tier)

**What it does:** Monitors competitor websites, social media, and ad campaigns. Delivers a weekly briefing on what changed.

**Why agencies need it:** Clients expect you to know their competitive landscape. Monitoring it manually is impossible at scale. Competitive Intelligence makes it automatic.

**ROI math:** Use it as a value-add for retained clients ("we monitor your competitors weekly") — justifies $500-$1,000/month more on retainer fees.
